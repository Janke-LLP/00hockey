Scott Janke — Coaching Background

Hockey Coach · Player Development Leader · Evaluator · Instructor · Tisdale, SK

Development-focused hockey coach and Hockey Saskatchewan Certified Power Skating Instructor with nearly three decades of experience as player, coach, on-ice facilitator, off-ice organizer, and player evaluator — across pre-season preparation, evaluation camps, roster selection and communication, in-season teaching, and the mental preparation athletes need for the next stage of hockey and life. Known for combining high expectations with encouragement, accountability with enjoyment, and skill development with character development.

Teach

Make complex concepts clear and actionable.

Develop

Build skills, confidence, hockey IQ and resilience.

Evaluate

Assess present performance and long-term potential.

Prepare

Help athletes succeed at the next stage of hockey and life.
